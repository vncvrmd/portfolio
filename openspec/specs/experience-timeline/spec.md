# experience-timeline Specification

## Purpose
Defines the campus events the Experience timeline credits the owner with heading.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Timeline names the events the owner headed
The Experience timeline's "Project Head & Lead Organizer" entry SHALL name these events: ROARientation and Welcome Walk 2024, Thomasian Welcome Party 2024, R101 2024 and 2025, Thomasian Youth Ambassador and Ambassadress 2025, MAKIBATA 2025, Secretariat 2024, Diamonds 2024, and SOCC's 50th Homecoming Anniversary. A separate bullet SHALL state "Lead Organizer and Documentation Head for UST Paskuhan 2024 and 2025." The entry MUST NOT mention Crank IT or Build IT.

#### Scenario: Visitor reads the events entry
- **WHEN** a visitor reads the "Project Head & Lead Organizer" entry in the Experience timeline
- **THEN** it lists the events above and the Paskuhan role, and neither "Crank IT" nor "Build IT" appears
